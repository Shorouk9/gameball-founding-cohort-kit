# Founding Cohort Launch Kit

An independent proposal by Shorouk for how to pick, onboard and prove success with the first founding brands of Gameball's new agentic loyalty product.

**Live page:** https://shorouk9.github.io/gameball-founding-cohort-kit/

## What's inside

- **Brand readiness check**: an interactive scorecard to choose which waitlist brands join the founding cohort.
- **The six-week pilot**: a week-by-week plan from kickoff to go/no-go decision, with owners and exit criteria.
- **Weekly rhythm**: how a small team keeps 6 to 8 pilots on track at once.
- **Success measures**: one main measure, supporting measures, guardrails and proposed targets.
- **Risks and plans**: what could go wrong and what to do about it.
- **Lessons from today's onboarding**: three failure points from public reviews and docs, and how the pilot designs around them.

## About

Built from public information only (company website, help docs, app store reviews, press coverage and the CEO's public LinkedIn post of 3 October 2026). All criteria, weights and targets are my own proposal.
